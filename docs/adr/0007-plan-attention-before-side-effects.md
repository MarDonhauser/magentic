# Plan attention before side effects

Attention policy is derived once from a coherent Observation plus explicit
break events, break advice, deployment outcomes, active-Session, and quiet
signals. The stateful planner owns confirmation, priority, cadence,
deduplication, and suppression; platform notification, Dock, native-attention,
and window calls only execute its intents. Direct UI events such as a finished
or reset break enter through the same planner instead of notifying on their own.
Unavailable runtime knowledge never means all Sessions are dead and never
clears a previously exact badge; a partial badge is rendered as a lower bound.
